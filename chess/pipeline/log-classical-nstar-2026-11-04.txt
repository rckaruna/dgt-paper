Classical (link-scale) D-study requirements for the chess response times,
read from the median curves saved by 03_analyze.R in chess_results.rds
(dstudy_curves$person_classical, $item_classical, $person_dgt), run on the
Mac on 4 Nov 2026 to check the counts quoted in Section 9.2. The last line
is a control: the DGT counts read the same way must equal the draw-wise
values printed by 03_analyze.R (26 and 15), and they do.

Command (from chess/pipeline/):
Rscript -e '
r <- readRDS("chess_results.rds"); cc <- r$rt$dstudy_curves
f <- function(cv, c) min(cv$n[cv$median >= c])
cat("classical person n*: 0.80 ->", f(cc$person_classical, .80), " 0.70 ->", f(cc$person_classical, .70), "\n")
cat("classical item   n*: 0.80 ->", f(cc$item_classical, .80),   " 0.70 ->", f(cc$item_classical, .70), "\n")
n80 <- f(cc$person_classical, .80)
cat("response-scale E rho^2 at n =", n80, ":", round(cc$person_dgt$median[cc$person_dgt$n == n80], 3), "\n")
cat("DGT person n*: 0.80 ->", f(cc$person_dgt, .80), " 0.70 ->", f(cc$person_dgt, .70), "\n")
'

Output:
classical person n*: 0.80 -> 20  0.70 -> 12 
classical item   n*: 0.80 -> 6  0.70 -> 4 
response-scale E rho^2 at n = 20 : 0.759 
DGT person n*: 0.80 -> 26  0.70 -> 15 

Section 9.2 therefore reads 20 (classical, persons, 0.80), 12, 6, 4, and a
response-scale reliability of 0.76 at n = 20. The submitted and the
29 Oct 2026 versions said 21 and 0.77, which matched nothing printed.
